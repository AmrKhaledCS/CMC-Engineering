About CMC

An engineering office working across civil, marine and coastal projects — study, design, documentation and supervision.

Who we are

One office for work on land and on the water

Engineering Office for Civil and Marine Works (CMC) is a multidisciplinary engineering consultancy founded in 2017, specializing in the design and supervision of civil, marine, coastal, structural, and geotechnical projects. CMC combines technical expertise across multiple engineering disciplines to deliver safe, efficient, innovative, and high-quality solutions, from initial design and analysis through construction supervision and project management.

Marine & Coastal Engineering — Design and analysis of marine structures, coastal protection systems, piers, breakwaters, and related infrastructure. Civil & Structural Engineering — Design of residential, commercial, high-rise, steel structures, and bridge projects. Geotechnical Engineering — Soil and foundation analysis, geotechnical assessment, and foundation system design. Construction Supervision & Project Management — Technical supervision, quality control, coordination, and project management throughout construction. Integrated Engineering Approach — Civil, marine, structural, hydraulic, and geotechnical disciplines are coordinated within one team to ensure consistent and practical project solutions.

Mission

To deliver innovative, reliable, and high-quality engineering solutions through technical excellence, practical expertise, and a commitment to safety and client satisfaction.

Vision

To become a trusted leader in civil, marine, coastal, and structural engineering, recognized for innovation, excellence, and lasting project value.

How we work

The habits behind the drawings

Checked twice

Calculations and drawings are reviewed by a second engineer against the agreed design codes before issue.

Dated revisions

Every drawing and report carries a revision, a date and a reason for change, so the record stays clear for everyone.

Site-aware design

Access, plant, tides and weather windows are considered while the design is still on the screen, not after mobilisation.

Documented handover

Projects close with as-built records, test results and maintenance notes rather than a box of loose files.

One point of contact

The engineer you meet first stays responsible for the project, so decisions and history are not lost between people.
