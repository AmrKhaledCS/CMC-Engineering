Engineering services

Six areas of work, each with the deliverables you can expect. Edit or remove any block — the headings match the dropdown in the main menu.

01 · Civil Engineering

Civil Engineering

Structural and site engineering for buildings, retaining works, roads, drainage and earthworks — from concept sizing to issued-for-construction drawings.

  • Structural analysis, calculations and detailing
  • Foundations, retaining structures and earthworks
  • Drainage, site levels and external works
  • Quantities, specifications and tender drawings

02 · Marine Engineering

Marine Engineering

Structures that work in and over water: quay walls, jetties, berthing and mooring systems, slipways and marina layouts, designed around vessel data and operating loads.

  • Quay walls, jetties and piled deck structures
  • Berthing, mooring and fendering arrangements
  • Slipways, ramps and small-craft facilities
  • Condition assessment and repair design

03 · Coastal Engineering

Coastal Engineering & Protection

Shoreline studies and protection works based on wave, tide and sediment transport analysis, with the effect on neighbouring stretches of coast assessed as part of the design.

  • Wave, tide and current analysis
  • Breakwaters, revetments, seawalls and groynes
  • Beach nourishment and shoreline management
  • Erosion and flooding risk assessment

04 · Infrastructure Design

Infrastructure Design

Roads, bridges, culverts and utility corridors, coordinated with topographic survey and with the services already in the ground.

  • Road geometry, pavement and drainage design
  • Bridges, culverts and crossing structures
  • Utility corridors and services coordination
  • Staging and traffic management during works

05 · Engineering Consultancy

Engineering Consultancy

Independent technical advice: feasibility studies, design review, second opinions, value engineering and reports for owners, insurers and authorities.

  • Feasibility and option studies
  • Design review and verification
  • Value engineering and constructability advice
  • Technical reports and expert input

06 · Project Management

Project Management & Technical Supervision

Support from tender through to handover: programme and cost monitoring, site inspection, quality control and close-out documentation.

  • Tender documents and contractor evaluation
  • Programme, cost and progress monitoring
  • Site inspection and quality control
  • Testing records, as-builts and handover
