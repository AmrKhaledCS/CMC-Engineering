What working with our office is like

Five things we hold ourselves to on every commission, whatever its size.

Engineering expertise

Civil, marine and coastal work handled by one office, so the structural, hydraulic and geotechnical sides of a project stay consistent with each other.

Quality and precision

Calculations, drawings and specifications are checked by a second engineer before they leave the office, against the design codes agreed with you.

Practical solutions

We look for the option that can actually be built with the plant, materials and access available on your site, then justify it with numbers.

Client-focused approach

One engineer stays responsible for your project from first meeting to handover, so you never have to re-explain the background.

Environmental care

Shoreline and nearshore works are assessed for their effect on sediment movement, water quality and neighbouring stretches of coast.

Our engineering process

How a project moves through the office

Four stages. You get a named engineer, a written scope and a drawing schedule at the end of the first one.

  1. 01

    Consultation & requirements

    We review your site data, constraints and objectives, agree the design codes, and set out the scope, programme and fee.

  2. 02

    Planning & engineering design

    Concept options are compared, then the chosen option is developed into calculations, drawings and specifications.

  3. 03

    Execution & technical supervision

    During construction we answer site queries, inspect the works and check that what is built matches the approved design.

  4. 04

    Delivery & quality review

    Final inspection, as-built records and a close-out report covering testing, snags and maintenance recommendations.
